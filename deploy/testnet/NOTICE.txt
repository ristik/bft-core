UNICITY PUBLIC TESTNET — TEST ASSETS HAVE NO REAL VALUE

UCT on this network is disposable gas for testing. This is not a TGE or allocation
of real assets. The operator may reset the network arbitrarily, without notice,
backward compatibility, migration or obligation. All balances, transactions and
assets may be lost. Never deposit real assets or reuse valuable keys.

Initially all validator entities, execution clients, aggregator, faucet and access
services share one bare-metal host. Host, disk, network or operator failure can
stop the entire testnet. Multiple validator containers do not create independent
physical failure domains. No production SLA or continuity promise is made.

There is one aggregator. The root trusts the single aggregator's signature;
it does not verify the aggregator's tree (no consistency proof).

If this generation runs the proof-of-stake profile (the manifest has a "pos" block): bonded stake, reward
credits and every balance in the staking, election and reward contracts are test value only. They vanish
with any reset and nothing is owed for them. The validator identities' owner, withdrawal and payee keys
are held by the operator. The contracts carry a development governance authority, held by the operator,
that may queue changes to the reward rate and the protection policy within fixed bounds, each taking
effect only after fixed delays; it cannot edit credits, payees or election candidates. Elections,
slashing and exits on this network exercise the mechanics; they are not an economic or security guarantee.
