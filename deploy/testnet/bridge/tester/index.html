Bridge test ether to Unicity tokens

Fake value. Expect to lose everything.

  • Bridged tokens are test ether locked in a test vault. They have no value, and no real asset can enter or leave through this bridge.
  • The operator may reset this network at any time, without notice or obligation. A reset loses every lock, bridged token and vault credit. Nothing carries over to another network or release, and nothing is compatible with a later one.
  • A bridged token exists only in your wallet file. Lose the file and the token is gone for good, and its test ether stays in the vault.
  • Nothing here is audited. Never use a key that holds anything of value.

The round trip: lock test ether in the vault on the EVM, mint the bridged token on the Unicity aggregator, transfer it, burn it, and redeem the burn in the vault. The vault then credits the address the burn names, which claims the ether back.

Steps

  1. Get the bridge tester: a checkout of native-bridge-plugins at the release's commit (npm ci), or the bridge tools image. Download tester-config.json and the four files it names into one directory: trust-base.bundle.cbor, trust-base.genesis.entryid, evm-pdr.cbor and bridge-deployment.json.
  2. npx tsx tester/bridge-tester.ts init --config tester-config.json
    Fund the EVM address it prints with test ether from the faucet.
  3. npx tsx tester/bridge-tester.ts lock --amount 0.005
npx tsx tester/bridge-tester.ts mint
    Mint soon after locking. The lock-proof endpoint serves a lock only for about 64 blocks after it.
  4. npx tsx tester/bridge-tester.ts transfer --count 2
npx tsx tester/bridge-tester.ts burn
    The burn returns the token to your wallet's EVM address. --recipient 0x… names another.
  5. npx tsx tester/bridge-tester.ts redeem
npx tsx tester/bridge-tester.ts claim
npx tsx tester/bridge-tester.ts status

Your wallet is the ./bridge-wallet directory. It holds your keys unencrypted, and your tokens.
