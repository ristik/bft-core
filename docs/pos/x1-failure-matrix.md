# X1 foundation failure matrix

Tracks [X1 #42](https://github.com/ristik/bft-core/issues/42) and its 2026-09-25 transferred list (parent clauses #11.4/.6/.8, #12.6, #13.3/.4/.7, #14.8, #10.7/.9). It connects each root, execution, certificate and transition failure to the test, model or run that establishes its behaviour, with the status of that evidence. It is a status record, not a gate decision. Independent review of this document is row X-43. The review is recorded on PR #351 and corrected rows in place; the rows it added are in section 11.

**Revisions.** Refreshed 2026-10-20 (X1 #42): bft-core `integration/enshrined-evm` at `a02be17ce` and ureth `unicity/main` at `6e59b68cc29f196d54d3d6e28d6266a8c02d05a1` (the ureth of the UTB lanes; it is on `unicity/main`). Every `file:line` reference to bft-core or ureth was re-resolved by script at the refreshed revisions (a Go test is found by `func <name>`, a Rust test by `fn <name>`; 73 bft-core test references and 20 ureth references checked, none missing; the lines of 29 Go and 20 Rust references had moved and were updated). The bft-go-base reference in X-42 and the `scripts/` references were not re-resolved by script. Rows whose evidence changed since the first version carry a **Refresh** note; the other rows keep their cited evidence, which was re-checked only as far as its test still exists at the new revisions. `file:line` references are resolved by script, not typed; a test body that calls `t.Skip` is never cited. M1 historical runs keep their own pins (the `m1-gate-evidence` release assets) and are not restated against this head. **Status.** MET: the stated behaviour is established by the cited evidence at the cited revision. MET-WITH-LIMIT: established, with the named limit. MISSING: no sufficient evidence; sized S (under a day), M (days), L (a week or more). Evidence kinds: *unit* (in-process test), *fixture* (real-execution fixture in ureth), *process* (child process, SIGKILL), *lane* (paired real ureth devnet), *model* (executable design model).

**Reproduce.** Go tests: `go test ./<pkg> -run '^<Name>$' -count=1` at the bft-core revision. Ureth tests: `cargo test -p reth-unicity-execution <fn>` for paths under `crates/unicity/execution`, `cargo test -p reth-unicity-payload <fn>` for `crates/unicity/payload`, at the ureth revision. The cited tests take no seed or random-input argument; their fixtures are fixed. Lane evidence directories live under `/Users/risto/uni/agre/briefs/devnet-runs/` and in the M1 release assets of `m1-gate-evidence`, **not in this repository** (see `docs/pos/m2-closure-status.md`); each run's pins and command are in its `run-provenance.md`, `run-pins.txt`, `commands.txt` or `lane.log` header.
