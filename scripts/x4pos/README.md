# X4 #58 step B: the resumable PoS scenario driver

Plan: `briefs/x4-pos-driver-plan.md`. Design: `briefs/x4-shadow-campaign-design.md` section 3. This directory is **B1: the manifest, the durable cursor and the preflight, with no chain**.

- `campaign.json` declares the four-hour experiment: interval 14,400 s with a reserved final hour, the policy windows of the P85 genesis (evidence window 1,000 and holds 2,000 rounds, UC-time floor 3,600 s: an exit matures after `max(3600, 2000 x round time) + safety` = 3,900 s), the required counts (2 replacements and key rotations, 1 stalled-successor recovery, 1 abort/retry, 2 offences, 2 exits, 4 reward intervals + exhaustion/refill, 4 faults, 1 governance change and its activation), and the 23 ordered steps with offsets, budgets, dependencies, exclusivity (faults, replacements, recovery and abort are serialised with a settle time so the intended quorum stays available) and idempotent `check` commands.
- `driver.py preflight` refuses a manifest whose schedule cannot finish (a step ending inside the final hold, a dependency that cannot have finished, an exit claim before maturity, exclusive steps closer than the settle time, a required count the plan does not yield). `driver.py run` additionally refuses a manifest whose steps have no implementation: nothing is stubbed into a pass. **As of B1 none of the step commands exist** (`scripts/x4pos/steps/*.sh` come with B2-B4), so `run` refuses the checked-in campaign, and says so.
- The cursor (every step declares its `check` or an explicit `check: null` with an `uncheckedReason` (only the faults, which leave no durable state; they are not restartable and the report lists them as unverified); `cursor.json` with a checksum, `events.jsonl`) is durable and is only ever a claim: on resume a step it calls done is verified by its `check`, an interrupted step is adopted only if its check shows the effect, re-run only if declared `restartable`, and otherwise fails; a changed manifest refuses to resume; a step finishing after its budget is a deadline failure; a campaign that stops is INCOMPLETE.

`python3 scripts/x4pos/test_driver.py` (20 tests, workflow `x4-pos-driver-selftest`).

**B5 (accounting checker and collector): `accounting.py`, `collect.py`.** `python3 scripts/x4pos/accounting.py check SNAPSHOTS.jsonl` reads periodic snapshots of the one accumulated state (schema in the file's docstring) and reports the FIRST failing invariant: monotone cursors, pool conservation (funded = paid + balance), custody conservation (**deposited = withdrawn + held**: in the pinned custody a slash leaves its lot as a bounty/treasury credit, so it is inside `held` until claimed and inside `withdrawn` after; the earlier `+ slashed` double counted it, corrected here; slashed <= deposited and balance >= held), no duplicate or rewritten payment, time/block order. Exit 0 PASS, 1 FAIL, 2 INCOMPLETE (under two snapshots). `collect.py snapshot` appends one snapshot read with `cast` at one pinned block (custody lots/totals/CreditClaimed, pool Funded/Claimed; names from the pinned contracts, listed in its docstring) and cross-checks `withdrawn` against the lots (exit 1 with a note on a mismatch). `driver.py report` merges the verdict when the manifest has `"accounting": true` (the real campaign does): FAIL fails the campaign, a missing or too short `snapshots.jsonl` keeps it INCOMPLETE, so it cannot PASS without the collector having run. **Limits, stated:** the collector and its test run against a stub `cast`; nothing has run against a live chain yet. Pool payments are one entry per Claimed event keyed by transaction and log index (the contracts name no interval in a claim), so the duplicate rule catches a repeated log, not a repeated economic payment. Only the `nextLotID` cursor is read. Offline tests: `test_accounting.py`, `test_collect.py`, `test_driver.py`, now all in the `x4-pos-driver-selftest` workflow. **Not built: the schedule of the collector inside a campaign run (a `lane` step), and the `x4-pos` ci-lane choice.**

**B6d (campaign.json v2, `lane` steps).** The campaign has two segments (decided with the orchestrator: a ~2 h 10 min lane segment and the driver segment, four hours declared). The **lane** steps (`"lane": true`, no `cmd`) are phases of the P85 recovery lane's own shell (baseline, the joiner install, the recovery, the held offender's exit request, offence 1, offence 2, the epoch switches, the rewards that the chain can show, the exit claim, the governance queue (right after the baseline) and its execute + outcome (late, after both delays), the accounting verdict). The driver never runs them: each is done iff `scripts/x4pos/lane-check.sh <phase>` finds the lane's record (`$X4_LANE_DIR/lane-steps.log`, written by `p85_step`) and the artifacts of the phase; they must come before the first driver step. The **driver** steps (four faults, reconcile) act on the same chain after the lane. `notCovered` lists, each with a pointer, what this campaign does not show: abort/retry on the accumulated state (bft-core#808 and the H6 abort lane), a second / ordinary replacement (joiner install + recovery instead, #777), an exit by a non-offender, governance ACTIVATION (queue and execute are shown; after execute + 1800 s the policy id was unchanged with an election result open, run 40071011875; cause per dev3's reading of the contracts, not observed: elect returns Disabled while a result is open), and a later reward interval paid after the refill / 4 intervals (the pool cannot close the current assignment's tail while an election result is open, unicity-pos-contracts#71, fixed in #72 which the pin does not include). **Not built:** the fault and reconcile step scripts, so `driver.py run` still refuses the manifest and says which; the lane step that hands the chain to the driver before the lane's teardown. `test_lane_check.py` pins each phase's check against a fixture of what a passing lane leaves.
